Code Rush

Technical Quiz

A fast-paced, two-round quiz competition exclusively on the Cyber Security domain. Test your knowledge, speed, and precision!

🧠 Think Fast. Answer Faster.

📅 Date 28 August 2026
⏰ Time 8:30 AM onwards
👥 Team Size 2 Members
💰 Registration Fee ₹150 per team

🛡️ Quiz Domain: Cyber Security

All questions in both rounds will be related to Cyber Security, including fundamental concepts, current trends, and practical applications.

Cyber Threats Digital Safety Ethical Hacking Network Security Cryptography Malware Cybersecurity Awareness

📋 Rules & Regulations

  1. The event is open to all eligible students.
  2. Each team shall consist of 2 members.
  3. All participating teams must complete their registration before the commencement of the event.
  4. Participants must arrive before 8:30 AM for registration and verification.
  5. Student ID card is mandatory.
  6. Mobile phones, smart watches, and electronic gadgets are prohibited.
  7. Any malpractice leads to immediate disqualification.
  8. All teams should bring their own stationery.
  9. All participants are expected to maintain discipline and display respectful behavior throughout the event.
  10. Judge's decision will be final.

📝 Round 1 — Preliminary Quiz

  1. Each team will receive a printed question paper containing multiple-choice questions (MCQs).
  2. Each question will have four answer options (A, B, C, D).
  3. Teams must complete the paper within the allotted time.
  4. Discussion between team members is allowed.
  5. The use of electronic devices, books, and internet access is strictly prohibited.
  6. The top-scoring teams will qualify for Round 2.
  7. In the event of a tie, additional tie-breaker questions or the fastest submission may be considered at the discretion of the judges.
30 Questions
20 min Duration
1 mark Per Question
None Negative Marking

🏆 Round 2 — Finals

  1. Questions will be displayed one by one on the projector screen.
  2. Each question will remain visible for 7 seconds only.
  3. After 7 seconds, the presentation will automatically move to the next question.
  4. No question will be repeated.
  5. Each team will be provided with an answer sheet.
  6. Teams must write only the corresponding option (A, B, C, or D) for each question.
  7. Answers must be written before the next question appears.
  8. Altering answers after the completion of the round is strictly prohibited.
20 Questions
7 sec Per Question
1 mark Per Question
None Negative Marking
