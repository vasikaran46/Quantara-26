📋 Rules & Guidelines

  1. Individual participation only.
  2. Choose only ONE theme from the four available options.
  3. Maximum prompt attempts allowed: 5 prompts. Every additional prompt beyond 5 will result in deduction of marks.
  4. Participants must submit: Screenshot of all prompts used in the AI tool, and the final AI-generated image.
  5. Prompt must include: Role + Task + Context + Output Format + Constraints.
  6. Only AI-generated content created during the event is allowed.
  7. No plagiarism or pre-generated images.
  8. Entries must be submitted on or before 5:30 PM, 26 August 2026.
  9. Results will be announced on 27 August 2026.
  10. Judges' decision is final.
