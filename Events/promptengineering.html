📋 Rules & Guidelines

  1. Each participant/team will be assigned one theme.
  2. The final image must be generated only during the event.
  3. Participants must use text prompts to create the image.
  4. Any AI image generation platform may be used.
  5. The submitted image must be original and relevant to the assigned theme.
  6. Participants must submit: the final AI-generated image and the exact prompt used to generate it.
  7. Pre-generated images are not allowed.
  8. Heavy editing or manipulation after image generation is prohibited.
  9. Any offensive, inappropriate, or copyrighted content will lead to disqualification.
