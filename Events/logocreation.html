📋 Rules & Regulations

  1. Only one entry per participant is allowed.
  2. Participants must complete the registration before the submission deadline.
  3. The logo must be the participant's original work.
  4. The logo should strictly follow the given theme.
  5. Logos must be submitted in PNG, JPG, or PDF format (high resolution).
  6. The logo should not contain copyrighted images, trademarks, or plagiarized content.
  7. AI-generated logos or copied templates without significant original modification are not permitted.
  8. The logo should not contain offensive, inappropriate, or discriminatory content.
  9. Logo submissions must be made through the official Google Form.
  10. Entries must be submitted on or before 5:30 PM, 26 August 2026.
  11. The decision of the judges will be final and binding.
  12. Results will be announced on 27 August 2026.
