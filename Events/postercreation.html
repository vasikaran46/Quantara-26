📋 Rules & Regulations

  1. Only one entry is allowed per participant.
  2. Participants must be officially registered for the event.
  3. The poster must be the participant's original work. Plagiarism will lead to immediate disqualification.
  4. The poster should strictly follow the given Theme (Cyber Security).
  5. Do not use copyrighted images, logos, or content without proper permission.
  6. The poster should not contain any offensive or inappropriate material.

📤 Submission Details

  1. Submit the poster in JPG, PNG, or PDF format.
  2. Posters can be submitted via the official Google Form provided to registered participants.
