Paper Presentation

Present research papers or innovative technical concepts in PPT format before an expert panel of judges. Showcase your knowledge, communication, and research skills.

🎤 Research. Present. Inspire.

📅 Date 28 August 2026
⏰ Time 8:30 AM – 2:00 PM
👥 Team Size 2 Members
💰 Registration Fee ₹150 per team

📝 Presentation Topics

Teams must select one topic from the list below for their presentation.

  1. Artificial Intelligence in Modern Life
  2. Cybersecurity in the Digital World
  3. The Future of Cloud Computing
  4. Role of Data Science in Business
  5. Smart Cities Using Modern Technology

📋 Event Rules

  1. Each team must consist of 2 members.
  2. Reporting time is before 8:30 AM. Team members must arrive on time for verification and registration.
  3. Participants must carry their College ID card.
  4. The presentation must be in PPT format.
  5. Total presentation time is 5–7 minutes, followed by 1–2 minutes for questions from the judges.
  6. The decision of the judges is final and binding.
  7. Any form of misconduct or plagiarism may lead to disqualification.

🎯 Presentation Format

  1. Teams must prepare their presentation in PowerPoint (PPT) format.
  2. Presentations should cover the chosen topic with clear structure: Introduction, Key Points, Analysis, and Conclusion.
  3. Slides should be visually clean, well-organized, and free from excessive text.
  4. Both team members are encouraged to participate in the presentation.
  5. A Q&A session will follow each presentation where judges may ask questions on the topic.
