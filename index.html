What is QUANTARA?

QUANTARA '26 is the flagship annual inter-college technical symposium organized by DEDOMENA, the Department Association of the Department of Computer Science with Data Science at Patrician College of Arts and Science, Chennai.

Built around the theme "Innovating Beyond Boundaries," the symposium brings together students from different institutions to compete in technical events, share ideas, develop practical skills, and celebrate innovation.

About DEDOMENA

DEDOMENA is the official student association of the Department of Computer Science with Data Science at Patrician College of Arts and Science. It serves as the academic and professional platform connecting students through technical, educational, and skill-development activities.

Activities

  • 🔬 Technical Workshops
  • 🎤 Guest Lectures
  • 🧭 Career Guidance Programs
  • 🏭 Industrial Visits
  • 💡 Skill Development Sessions
  • ⌨️ Coding Activities
  • 🏆 Technical Competitions
  • 🎓 Symposiums like QUANTARA

Department Vision

To empower students with the knowledge and skills required to excel in the rapidly evolving field of data science while fostering innovation, critical thinking, and data-driven decision-making.

Department Mission

  • 📚 Delivering comprehensive education in CS & Data Science
  • 🔧 Preparing graduates to solve real-world data challenges
  • ⚖️ Promoting ethical and responsible use of technology
  • 🌟 Developing technically competent professionals ready for industry
